## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Audio EQ** is a powerful audio equalizer extension for Google Chrome that allows you to modify sound frequencies across all HTML5 music players. Whether you're listening to music on YouTube or any other service, the **latest version** of Audio EQ is designed to enhance your audio experience by providing you with full control over the high, medium, and low frequencies. This **complete functionality** ensures that your sound is perfectly tailored to your preferences.
